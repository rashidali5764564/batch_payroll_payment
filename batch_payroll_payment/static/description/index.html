Batch Payroll Payment Module Overview

The Batch Payroll Payment module is a powerful Odoo extension designed for HR departments and Accounting teams who need to process multiple employee payroll payslips efficiently. This module eliminates the tedious task of processing individual payslip payments one by one, allowing you to pay multiple employees simultaneously using Odoo's standard payment register wizard.

Whether you're managing payroll for a small business or a large enterprise, this module streamlines your payment workflow, reduces processing time, and minimizes errors. Perfect for companies that process monthly, bi-weekly, or weekly payroll payments in bulk.
